pl_mpeg - MPEG-1 video decoder, MP2 audio decoder, MPEG-PS demuxer
by Dominic Szablewski (https://phoboslab.org)
https://github.com/phoboslab/pl_mpeg

The launcher (RR6 Launcher.exe) contains a copy of pl_mpeg with two small
changes, described at the top of launcher\pl_mpeg_sofdec.h in the source. It
is used to read still pictures from the opening movie of the player's own copy
of the game.
