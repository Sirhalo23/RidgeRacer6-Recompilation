You normally do not need this folder: start "RR6 Launcher.exe", press
"Choose disc image..." and pick your .iso wherever it is.

This folder is for "Play without the launcher.bat", the fallback for when the
launcher will not open:

- Copy your own Ridge Racer 6 (USA) Xbox 360 disc image (.iso) into this folder.
- Exactly one .iso file should be in here. The file name does not matter.
- Then go back up one folder and double-click "Play without the launcher.bat".

It copies the game files out of the .iso once (about 6 GB, a few minutes).
After that the .iso is no longer read; you can move it away again.
